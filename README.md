## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn and revenue loss in a telecom company using Tableau and SQL.  
The goal of the analysis was to identify:

- which customers are most likely to churn
- when churn happens most often
- which customer groups generate the biggest revenue loss
- what factors may influence customer retention

The project combines KPI tracking, churn segmentation, revenue analysis, and visualizations to transform customer data into actionable insights.

---

## Dataset

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn/data

The project uses IBM’s sample telecom customer dataset designed for churn analysis and customer retention research.

The dataset simulates customer behavior in a subscription-based telecom business and includes information related to customer contracts, payment methods, tenure, monthly charges, and churn behavior.

---

## Data Preparation & Transformation

Data preparation and transformation included:

- handling missing values
- creating calculated fields for churn metrics
- calculating MRR, ARPU, and Lost MRR
- grouping customers by duration
- validating calculations in SQL

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Tableau Public
- SQL
- Data Visualization
- Business Analysis

---

## Key Insights

- Customers with month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate (~43%)
- Nearly half of customer churn happens during the first year
- Around 87% of total revenue loss comes from month-to-month customers
- Customers using electronic check payments show significantly higher churn rates
- Longer contracts are associated with much stronger customer retention

---

## Recommendations

- Encourage customers to switch to longer contracts
- Improve experience for new customers
- Analyze why customers using electronic check payments have higher churn rates
- Focus retention campaigns on high-risk customer segments

---

## Conclusion

The analysis showed that contract type and customer duration are strongly connected to churn behavior and revenue loss.

This project demonstrates how Tableau dashboards and business analysis can help identify retention risks, measure financial impact, and support data-driven decision-making.
